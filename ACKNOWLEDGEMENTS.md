# Origins and acknowledgements

Evidence Writer grew out of an internal Chinese nonfiction writing workflow
developed through repeated production use, independent review, and regression
testing.

Its direct predecessors were a set of custom AI writing skills and workflow
components for research, evidence auditing, writer handoff, author intent,
capability selection, drafting, and final review. These components were later
redesigned as explicit software contracts and implemented as a standalone
runtime.

During development, public AI writing tools, agent skills, and writing systems
were studied for comparison, problem framing, and workflow design. Evidence
Writer v0.1 does not vendor or fork third-party prompt files or runtimes unless
explicitly stated.

The writing capability system evolved from an internal Reference Library that
analyzes transferable writing mechanisms from published nonfiction works.
Production capabilities are abstracted mechanisms rather than author-style
templates: author names, style-cloning instructions, and copyrighted prose are
not used as runtime capabilities.

Third-party software dependencies retain their respective licenses.

If future provenance review identifies third-party code, prompts, or other
material that requires attribution or additional license notices, the relevant
credits and notices will be added explicitly.
